# Central Limit Theorem Monte Carlo Investigation

## Project Overview

This project investigates the Central Limit Theorem using Monte Carlo simulation in R.

I simulated 10,000 sample means for sample sizes of 1, 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, and 100 from five different populations:

- Standard Normal
- Six-Sided Die
- Exponential
- Small Binomial
- Cauchy

The sampling distributions were examined using histograms, normal Q-Q plots, simulated means, simulated standard deviations, and theoretical standard errors.

## Main Findings

The simulations showed that:

- Standard Normal sample means were approximately normal even for small sample sizes.
- Six-Sided Die sample means became approximately normal as the sample size increased.
- Exponential and Small Binomial populations required larger sample sizes because they were skewed.
- The Cauchy population demonstrated that the usual CLT does not apply when a population does not have a finite mean and variance.
- For populations with finite variance, the simulated standard deviations generally approached the theoretical standard errors.
